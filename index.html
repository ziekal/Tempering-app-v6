Criteria for Tempering

  • All windows with glass within 18 inches of floor and over 9 SQFT as shown in red.
  • Any window glass within 24 inches of door slab and 60 inches or less of the floor. (SQFT N/A)
  • Any window with a 60 inch radius of stairs and within 36 inches of the floor. (This applies to exterior stairs.)
  • Any window in a bathroom within 60 inches of the wet zone and less than 60 inches from the ground. (This applies to exterior pools and hot tubs.)
